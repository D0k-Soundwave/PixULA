## The menus

![The File menu, open](img/menu-file.png)

*The File menu: everything that comes in or goes out, plus the four editors.*

![The Image menu, open](img/menu-image.png)

*The Image menu, where the screen mode is set.*

Three items work on a selection. **Edit > Fit Selection to Cells** grows it to the whole attribute cells it touches. **Edit > New Picture from Selection** makes the selection the whole picture (see Files). **File > Save Selection As** saves just the selection (see Files). **View > Preview** shows or hides a small map of the whole picture: the frame is the part you see, and clicking or dragging on the map, or using its arrow keys, scrolls the canvas there. The map is off until you switch it on.
