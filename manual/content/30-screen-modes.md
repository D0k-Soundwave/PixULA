## Screen modes

A screen mode sets three things: how many pixels the picture has, how finely
colour is stored beside them, and which palette those colours come from.

Change mode from the Image menu or from the selector in the status bar.
Your artwork is converted, not discarded. If the conversion will lose
something - a narrower palette, coarser colour, fewer pixels - you are
told before it happens.

One undo reverses a mode change, artwork included.
